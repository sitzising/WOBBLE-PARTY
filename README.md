# 🎉 WOBBLE PARTY

**Race. Build. Sabotage. Wobble your way to victory!**

WOBBLE PARTY is a chaotic physics-based party game where players race through unpredictable obstacle courses, build their own shortcuts, sabotage rivals, and survive ridiculous physics disasters.

Every race is different. Every shortcut is risky. And no plan survives the chaos.

## 🎮 Features

- **Physics-Based Gameplay** — Experience unpredictable movement, collisions, and hilarious physics interactions.
- **Race & Compete** — Rush through obstacle-filled courses and fight for first place.
- **Build Your Own Path** — Move objects, stack platforms, and create shortcuts.
- **Sabotage Your Rivals** — Push, grab, throw, and disrupt other players.
- **Dynamic Obstacles** — Survive moving platforms, traps, environmental hazards, and unexpected events.
- **Unpredictable Chaos** — Physics-driven moments make every match unique.

## 🏗️ Gameplay

Race through challenging courses, use objects around you to create new routes, and turn the environment into your advantage.

You can play it safe, build a shortcut, or ruin everyone's plan.

**Build your path. Wobble your way to victory!**
